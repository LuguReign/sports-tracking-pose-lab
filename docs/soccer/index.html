Research design

Match 1 trains the models, Match 2 sets empirical uncertainty radii, and Match 3 evaluates the frozen methods. Player mappings are read from metadata, including substitutions. History-only and interaction models use the same training match and fixed tree settings.

Three matches and one held-out test match support a methodological pilot. Repeated frames are dependent. This analysis does not demonstrate causal defensive pressure, scouting validity, skeletal pose estimation, or professional coaching recommendations.

Complete focal history and target positions are required; missing contextual tracking has validity indicators. Goalkeepers and stoppages are included. Models predict endpoint displacement rather than complete future paths.
