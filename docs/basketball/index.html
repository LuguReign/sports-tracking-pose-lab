Assumptions and limitations

Joint coordinates are measurements, not biomechanical ground truth. Complete selected markers are required after interpolation of internal gaps up to three frames. Smoothing sensitivity at nominal 0.10, 0.15 and 0.25 seconds is documented in the outputs.

The phase anchor is approximate; repeated shots can be serially dependent. Five athletes cannot support broad population or professional-sport claims. Session changes confound date, sampling rate, and pose model. No causal effect, optimal technique, or injury-risk claim is made.
